# All Saints Fermentery

> *Brewing beers in honor of the saints we venerate in Catholicism.*

| Founded | Rooted in | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2025 | Catholic feast-day tradition | Gathering, hospitality, and catechesis |

## The Idea

All Saints Fermentery began as an idea in 2025: brewing beers in honor of the saints we venerate in Catholicism.

The Catholic faith is incredibly rich in history and in the mysteries we celebrate. In the past, Christians celebrated feast days with food, wine, and beer. This project aims to help bring that tradition back because it is fundamentally important for us to gather rather than remain isolated, while continuing to cultivate and educate people about our faith.

## Why the Saints?

The saints are a perfect example of those who consecrated their lives to God and gave everything they had.

All Saints Fermentery is a tribute to these brave men and women who remained steadfast, even in the face of death; for they knew that death was not the end, because Christ Himself conquered sin and death.
